Sticky headers

Mark sticky or fixed chrome with txOverlayInset. While it is pinned, panels open clear of it and slide under it like page content. Once open, a panel rides with its origin while the page scrolls; scrolling a container of the origin, such as a dialog body, closes it. This site marks its header and the tab bar of component pages.

Stacking

Every overlay sits in the browser top layer, the newest on top, so a menu, a dropdown or a tooltip opened inside a dialog shows above it. A modal dialog closes the open popovers, menus, dropdown panels and tooltips when it opens, and toasts move back above it. TxOverlay panels and cdkConnectedOverlay panels with txOverlayAnchor and a (detach) handler take part by themselves; register any other panel with TxOverlayStack.addTransient(close).
