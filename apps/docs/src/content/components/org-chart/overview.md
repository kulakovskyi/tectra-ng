# Org Chart

A hierarchy as a tree of cards on a pan and zoom canvas with search, a minimap, full screen, hotkeys and the path to the root on hover. Cards come from the kit; a template draws your own.

Part of `@tectra-ng/charts` (`npm install @tectra-ng/charts`, also included in `tectra-ng`).
