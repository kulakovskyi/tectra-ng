# Wizard

A full-screen step-by-step flow for onboarding and setup: the step with its progress, heading, content and footer on the left, an illustrated stage that slides its scenes on the right.

A block from `@tectra-ng/blocks` — it comes with `tectra-ng`, or alone: `npm install @tectra-ng/blocks`. The examples sit in a 560px screen; in an app the wizard fills the window.
