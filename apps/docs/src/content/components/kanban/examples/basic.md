A task board built from the slots: a section header with a count, an add button and a menu; task cards with a check, pills, an assignee, a due date and counters; "Add task" in the footer and "Add section" after the last column, both turning into a quick-add field (Enter adds, Esc cancels). Drag cards and sections, or focus a card and press Alt with an arrow key — `focusableCards` is off because the card takes the focus itself. `itemDropped` and `columnDropped` give the new neighbours; `moveItem` / `moveColumn` apply them to the state.
